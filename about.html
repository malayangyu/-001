How it started

Small beginnings, a clear idea

CROSSUM is a Hong Kong, China based company supplying clothing and jewelry to customers around the world. We care about considered fabrics, better cuts and pieces with a little personality — without the noise.

Today we work with independent workshops across twelve countries. We keep runs small, check every detail, and only release a piece when we would happily wear it ourselves.
